## Training and payment

Nick will provide the training sessions identified above. Monthly packages run for one month from the client’s start date. Appointments are arranged separately; a recurring day or time is not part of this agreement. Nick may bring training equipment, and available equipment at the client’s home may also be used. Both participants are required for partner sessions. Partner training is shared equally and is not a discounted substitute for an individual session.

The full purchase price is due before the first session. Payment may be made by cash, check or Zelle. The app does not itself process these payments. The client’s acknowledgment of this agreement is not evidence of payment.

The package does not renew automatically. Any later package requires a new purchase and agreement. A client may change from partner to individual training only after the current partner plan ends. Nick will consider any unused sessions at the end of the term case by case, subject to applicable law. No automatic renewal is implied. ## Scheduling, cancellations and grace

A client who gives at least 24 hours’ notice may reschedule without using a session. For a partner session missed without at least 24 hours’ notice, the shared session counts as used for both partners unless Nick grants the absent partner a grace exception, subject to applicable law. A partner session does not proceed as an individual session when one partner is absent. If a client arrives late, the appointment ends at its scheduled time and the missed portion is lost.

Nick may grant each client one grace exception per package, based on the circumstances. A family emergency may qualify; an avoidable scheduling conflict may not. Granting an exception is Nick’s decision, and it is recorded in the app. An individual client granted grace keeps the session for rescheduling. If an absent partner is granted grace, both partners keep the shared session for rescheduling; only the absent partner uses their grace exception. Each partner has their own one-time grace exception for the package.

Repeated partner no-shows may lead Nick to decline or cancel a future package. This provision does not automatically cancel an existing purchase or authorize a forfeiture.
