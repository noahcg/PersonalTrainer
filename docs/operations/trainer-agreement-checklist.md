## 2. Policies to approve or change

These are proposed business choices. Required client protections remain part of the final agreement.

- [ ] **1 — Payment timing:** Clients pay before the first session. You can approve installments, with exact amounts and dates written into that client's agreement.
- [ ] **2 — Late payment:** You may pause future sessions after notifying the client. No late fees or interest.
- [ ] **3 — Client cancellations:** At least **24 hours' notice** means no lost session. Less notice or a no-show uses one session, without an extra fee. You may waive this for illness or emergencies.
- [ ] **4 — Late arrivals:** The session may still end at the originally scheduled time.
- [ ] **5 — Your cancellations:** The client chooses a replacement session or a refund for that session.
- [ ] **6 — Client stops training:** Refund unused sessions at their purchased rate. Do not increase the rate for sessions already used. Any more favorable required refund rules take priority.
- [ ] **7 — Package ends:** Use a clear end date. Refund unused paid sessions unless you and the client agree to an extension beforehand. **This also applies to monthly packages.**
- [ ] **8 — Renewals:** No automatic renewal. Each new package or month needs a new agreement accepted by the client.
- [ ] **9 — Client moves:** Agree on any new travel arrangements and price first. If you cannot serve the new address, refund unused services without a moving fee.
- [ ] **10 — Partners, if offered:** Each person owes only their own share. A session can proceed with one person. The absent person's credit follows the 24-hour policy; the attending person does not pay the other's share. Mark “later” if not offered.
- [ ] **11 — Your app workflow:** Review and approve each personalized agreement before sending it. The client then agrees in the app, and both of you receive a copy.
