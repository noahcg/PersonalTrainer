## Proposed purchase terms

Each agreement must identify the client and selected package, its price, session count, duration and purchase term. Do not embed recurring appointment days or times. The confirmed individual monthly offers are $840 for 8 one-hour sessions (twice weekly) and $550 for 4 one-hour sessions (once weekly). Partner training is $640 per partner per month, $1,280 combined, for four one-hour shared sessions. Training attention is shared equally; the partners may decide how to arrange payment. Monthly packages run one month from the client’s start date. The trainer plans to explain during the initial meeting that clients are responsible for using sessions within the allotted time; the user does not currently intend that explanation to be contract text. Nick will consider unused sessions case by case, subject to applicable law; do not implement automatic expiration or carryover. Legal review must resolve any required client rights and procedures.

Payment is due in full upfront, before training, by cash, check or Zelle. Packages do not renew automatically. A new purchase requires an affirmative agreement; existing paid terms are not changed by this draft.

## Proposed policies for legal and business review

- Nick Glushien provides training individually. The proposed policy is no refunds, subject to applicable law and any rights that cannot be waived. Counsel must determine the required notices, cancellation rights and refund calculations; this draft does not settle them.
- A late arrival does not extend the scheduled session; the missed portion is lost. With at least 24 hours’ notice, individual and partner sessions can be rescheduled without using a session.
- A partner session requires both participants and is not a discounted solo session. If one partner misses without at least 24 hours' notice, the shared session counts as used for both unless Nick grants the absent partner grace, subject to applicable law.
- Each client may receive one grace exception per package, at Nick’s discretion based on the circumstances. A family emergency may qualify; poor planning for a concert may not. Record the decision and reason in the app. An individual client granted grace keeps the session for rescheduling. When a partner is granted grace, both partners keep the shared session and only the absent partner uses their exception.
- Repeated partner no-shows may lead Nick to decline or cancel a future package; no automatic forfeiture of an existing package is authorized.
- A client cannot switch from partner to individual training mid-plan. The current plan must end before changing plan type. Any handling of unused sessions or financial adjustment requires explicit confirmation.

Nick may bring some equipment, such as TRX straps, and a client’s home may have equipment as well.

## Availability and operations

Proposed standard availability, in Nick's configured local time zone: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday starts at 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM; Sunday 8:00 AM–2:00 PM; Wednesday and Saturday unavailable. Sunday sessions must end by 2 PM. The calendar currently records individual appointments with a chosen time zone and duration (default 60 minutes); it has no recurring availability, booking window, or exception engine. Existing bookings must be preserved. The calendar shows these hours as a reference; it does not enforce them.

A trainer-only notice log records the session time, notice time or absence of notice, details, and a discretionary grace decision. It enforces one grace record per client per package, but does not change balances or link a notice to a scheduled appointment. Individual balances currently count all completed client sessions across the client’s history, not per package; they must be reconciled before treating them as authoritative. There is no payment ledger or agreement acceptance system. Do not represent a signed agreement as payment, or an existing client as having consented. Record offline receipt separately before training; build an authorized, auditable manual payment workflow before relying on app enforcement. Notice and grace records require review before session accounting is automated.

## Implemented, disabled agreement workflow

The application has a disabled-by-default agreement workflow (`AGREEMENTS_ENABLED=false`). After the database migration is applied and the reviewed workflow is explicitly enabled, Nick can offer an individually approved, text-selectable PDF from the client profile. He must verify that the PDF identifies the correct client, selected package, price and term; the app cannot determine whether the legal contents are correct. The server checks the package belongs to that client and trainer, stores a SHA-256 digest and immutable PDF/package snapshot, and permits one outstanding offer per client. The client sees only their own offer after intake and can review its PDF, open or download it, and submit a typed name, electronic-record acknowledgment, and unchecked-by-default agreement acknowledgment. The transaction records server time and the exact offered snapshot, prevents duplicate or stale acceptance, and prevents modifying acceptance records. Nick can withdraw an unsigned offer and view acceptance details and the original PDF. Signed agreements are never inferred for existing clients; Nick must create a separate offer for each client.

The PDF reader renders page images and exposes extracted page text to assistive technology. Text extraction is not a substitute for checking the final PDF's reading order, tagging, tables, and accessibility with a screen reader. The scroll control helps with navigation but does not prove that a person read the document. The electronic-record checkbox is a draft disclosure and must be reviewed, alongside required notices, document delivery, copy/retention requirements, and the final agreement, before any offer. The application currently provides the original PDF and the acceptance record separately; it does not generate or email a signed PDF. Session balances and payment receipt remain separate. The feature is not ready to deploy or enable until these gaps and full database, authorization, accessibility and end-to-end tests are resolved. Apply `supabase/client-agreements-migration.sql` before enabling, and do not turn the flag off after launch without providing another way for clients to retrieve their records.
