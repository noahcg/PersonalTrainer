## Trainer review: recommended decisions

| Topic | Recommended starting policy |
| --- | --- |
| Payment | Full payment before the first session; allow an explicitly documented installment schedule as an exception. |
| Renewal | Each purchase covers only the stated package or training period; no automatic renewal. |
| Client cancellation | At least 24 hours' notice: reschedule without losing a session. Less notice or a no-show: count one session, with trainer discretion to waive. |
| Trainer cancellation | Reschedule without charge or refund that unused session. |
| Refunds | Refund unused sessions at the price actually paid per session, without repricing used sessions at a higher rate. |
| Session expiration | State a definite contract term. Refund unused paid sessions at its end; any extension must be agreed in writing within applicable term limits. |
| Late payment | No late fees or interest. Trainer may pause future training after notifying the client. |
| Shared training | Each client signs for a clearly stated individual amount; no implied responsibility for the other client's share. |
| Signing | Trainer approves the completed offer; client reviews the same version in the app, checks an initially unchecked agreement box, and selects Agree and sign. Both parties' actions are recorded and both get a copy. |

These choices affect revenue and scheduling. In particular, no automatic renewal and unused-session refunds are proposed changes that must be reconciled with the trainer's monthly-package business model. California cancellation rights take priority over the ordinary missed-session policy.

### 3. Payment

You agree to pay the remaining amount by the dates shown above. Unless an installment schedule is included, payment is due before your first session. Signing this agreement does not charge a payment method or confirm receipt of payment.

If a payment is overdue, the trainer may pause future training after notifying you. No late fees or interest apply under this agreement. Any refund or outstanding balance after cancellation will be calculated under section 5; future installments will not be collected for refunded or canceled unused services.

### 4. Scheduling and missed sessions

Session times must be confirmed by the trainer. To cancel or reschedule, use the app's appointment cancellation function or contact the trainer through an app message or the contact details above.

With at least 24 hours' notice before the scheduled start time, you may reschedule without using a session. A cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, or failure to attend, counts as one used session. The trainer may waive this deduction for illness, emergencies, or other circumstances and will record that decision. There is no additional cancellation fee.

If you arrive late, the session may still end at the scheduled time. If the trainer cancels or cannot deliver the session, you may reschedule without using a session or request a refund for that session.

For partner training, a confirmed shared session may proceed with one participant. A participant who gives at least 24 hours' notice keeps their unused session credit; a late cancellation or no-show uses only that participant's credit. An alternative appointment or refund will be arranged for unused credits. The remaining participant will not be charged the absent participant's share.

### 5. Ending training, unused sessions, and refunds

Either party may end future training. Clients may give cancellation notice in person, by first-class mail, by email from their email address on file, or through the app. Statutory cancellation rights described below override ordinary missed-session deductions and payment terms wherever they provide greater protection.

Unused sessions are refundable. For an ordinary voluntary cancellation, the proposed calculation uses the total agreed session price divided by purchased sessions, deducting delivered sessions and otherwise valid missed-session charges from payments received. Apply any legally required calculation instead if it produces a larger refund or smaller remaining obligation. No missed-session charge may reduce a statutory refund unless legally permitted. Any separate non-session service must have its price and refund treatment expressly stated before signing.

[Review item: finalize separate statutory refund calculations and installment treatment before client use. Do not implement the ordinary session formula as the only refund rule.]

Used sessions will not be repriced at a higher rate because you end training early. Refunds will be made within 10 days after receipt of cancellation notice, or sooner when required. Death/disability refunds described below will be made immediately upon request. Payment processing does not extend applicable deadlines.

At the stated contract end date, unused paid sessions will be refunded within 10 days unless both parties have already agreed to a lawful written extension. If the trainer can no longer provide the purchased service, the unused portion will be refunded.

### 5A. California cancellation protections — draft summary

You have an initial cancellation right through midnight of the fifth business day after the agreement date, excluding Sundays and holidays. Additional price-based rights are 20 days for $1,500–$2,000, 30 days for $2,001–$2,500, and 45 days for $2,501 or more. Applicable rights must appear in the final agreement. Required notices, service availability, and statutory refund rules remain controlling. [Civil Code § 1812.85](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1812.85.)

If death or a physician-verified disabling condition prevents receipt of services, the client or estate owes no payment for services not received, and prepaid unused services will be refunded immediately upon request. If you move more than 25 miles from the training facility and cannot transfer to a comparable facility, unused services will be refunded and future payment obligations for those services end. No relocation cancellation fee applies under this agreement. [Civil Code § 1812.89](https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-civ/division-3/part-4/title-2-5/section-1812-89/)

## Research note

California defines electronic signatures to include a process associated with a record and adopted with intent to sign. The proposed checkbox plus explicit submission uses that model; validity still depends on attribution, consent, the agreement, and applicable requirements. Sources: [Civil Code § 1633.2(h)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1633.2.) and [§ 1633.7](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1633.7.).

California's definition includes exercise instruction; the working inference is that Nick's personal-training agreements should be designed for this title unless a California lawyer determines an exception applies. Having three clients who are friends is not an exemption identified in that definition. [Civil Code § 1812.81](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1812.81.).

The previous generic draft's 14-day refund proposal and portal-link-only delivery plan have been replaced. The final document also needs conspicuous term disclosure and lawful payment/contract duration; the reviewed § 1812.84 text limits terms to three years. [California Legislature, AB 2810 text amending § 1812.84](https://www.leginfo.ca.gov/pub/15-16/bill/asm/ab_2801-2850/ab_2810_bill_20160816_enrolled.html). This is a drafting review, not a completed legal compliance audit.

For a U.S. implementation, the federal E-SIGN Act recognizes electronic records and signatures but preserves underlying substantive obligations. Section 7001(c) addresses consumer consent where legally required written disclosures are provided electronically; section 7001(d) addresses accurate, accessible retained records. This does not settle state-specific fitness-service contract requirements or the enforceability of this draft.

Source: [15 U.S.C. § 7001, U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A15+section%3A7001+edition%3Aprelim%29), reviewed September 18, 2026.
